Iron & Oak

Place your troops, press Start battle, then command them in real time. Small armies can win through position, not numbers.

  • Fog: you only see what your troops can see. Scouts see furthest and spot troops hidden in forests. The enemy is just as blind.
  • Commander: his banner steadies nearby troops. If he falls, the army shakes.
  • Objective: choose one in the deploy panel. The enemy plan is different every battle.
  • Planning: scout the enemy, read the briefing, dig in, then deploy. The Plan button in the bottom bar lists the steps; Back and Next move between them.
  • Deploy: open Roster in the bottom bar, tap a unit, then tap your zone (bottom, blue). Tap a squad to select it, then tap the zone to move it.
  • Battle: tap your squad, then tap ground to move or an enemy to attack. Use the bar along the bottom for orders, formations and firing modes.
  • The field is huge: the armies start at opposite ends, so expect a long march. Use the minimap (bottom right) to jump around, and View in the bottom bar to return to your army or see everything. Hit 2× or 3× speed to speed up the walk.
  • Camera: drag to pan, pinch or use the wheel to zoom.
  • Terrain: tap bare ground to see what it is. Forests hide troops; mud, marsh, rock and fields slow movement; roads are fast; hills see further and shoot further; walls and buildings block movement, arrows and sight.
